Privacy Policy

Updated 4 November 2026

Information you share

When you sign in with Google we receive your account ID, name and email. If you sign up with an email or phone number and password, we store that login and a protected (hashed) copy of the password, never the password itself. We store your profile, posts, uploaded photos and videos, messages, groups, deals, reports and appeals so the website can work. If you apply for a badge, we also store your NID photo, WhatsApp number and the location you share at that moment; only the Owner can see them.

What we use to arrange the feed

To show popular posts first, WizeNex notes which posts signed-in members stay on, open, watch, like or comment on. This is only used to order the feed. Nobody, including other members, can see who looked at what.

Who can see it

Your name and photo appear on your posts. Full profiles need sign-in. Private chats are seen only by the people in them. Group posts and chats are seen by group members. A deal chat is seen by the buyer, the seller and the deal Admin. The Owner can see member login emails and handles reports, safety checks, suspensions and appeals.

Calls and alerts

Voice and video calls go directly between devices when possible, or through a relay server; calls are not recorded. If you turn on call alerts, your browser gives us a notification address used only to tell you about calls and missed calls.

Hosting and storage

Google handles Google sign-in. Posts and some images are checked by an automated safety service. Cloudflare hosts the website and stores records and files. Photos and videos may be made smaller on your phone before upload. Cookies keep you signed in; browser storage remembers settings such as hidden banners. The app does not keep private messages for offline use.

Your controls

You can edit your profile, delete your posts, block members, leave groups, and archive or delete your copy of a conversation. Other people in the chat and deal records keep their own copy.

Contact

For account or privacy questions, message the Owner, Rahat wize, on WizeNex, or use Help & support in the profile menu.
