Privacy Policy

Updated 30 October 2026

Information you share

Google sign-in provides your account identifier, name and email. We store your chosen profile details, posts, uploaded media, messages, deal activity and reports to operate the marketplace.

Who can see it

Your public name and photo appear on public posts. Full profile pages require sign-in. Private conversations are limited to their participants. Secure deal conversations are available to their participants and assigned Admin. The owner can access member login emails and manage reports, moderation and website permissions.

Hosting and storage

Google handles sign-in. Posts and selected uploaded images are checked by an automated safety service. Cloudflare hosts the website and stores application records and uploaded files. Public profile pictures and group logos may be cached to load faster. Authentication cookies keep you signed in; browser storage remembers preferences and app-install dismissal. The app does not cache private messages or account API responses for offline use.

Your controls

You can edit your profile, remove your posts, block private contact, and archive or delete your own conversation history. Deleting a chat affects your copy; other participants and existing deal records retain their own history. Post view tracking is disabled.

Contact the owner

For account or privacy questions, contact Rahat wize through WIZE MARKET.
